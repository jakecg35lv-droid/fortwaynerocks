Full Color Cobbles

Naturally rounded stones featuring a blend of tans, browns, grays, and earth tones. Available in seven size ranges for any project scale.

Full Color Cobbles are commonly used for landscape beds, drainage areas, and decorative accents. They provide strong visual texture and a natural, low-maintenance finish. Durable and versatile, full color cobbles add warmth and dimension to both residential and commercial outdoor spaces. Available in bulk or palletized.

Hoosier Select Stone  /  Bluffton

Bluffton Rock

Quarried in and around Bluffton, Indiana. Known for its durability and neutral gray tones — one of our top-selling products for landscape and hardscape projects across the Midwest.

Bluffton Rock, commonly referred to as Bluffton Limestone, is a natural stone with consistent quality and broad applications. Available in several popular forms including web wall, outcropping, thin random, wallstone, and blue split. Suitable for retaining walls, patios, walkways, and landscape accents.

Available Types

Web Wall Outcropping Blue Thin Random Buff Thin Random Wallstone Blue Split

Product Types

Six forms. One great stone.

Web Wall

Irregular, web-shaped pieces designed for dry-stack or mortared walls. Rugged natural look with strong visual texture for retaining walls and landscape features.

Outcropping

Large, layered pieces with a rugged natural look. Used for retaining walls, landscape accents, and slope stabilization to create an authentic ledge-rock appearance.

Blue Thin Random

Cool blue-gray tones in thin, irregular pieces. Used for patios, walkways, and landscape accents with a clean, natural look.

Buff Thin Random

Warm gray and buff tones in thin, irregular pieces. A classic look that blends easily into Midwest outdoor spaces for patios and walkways.

Wallstone

Used for retaining walls, garden walls, and landscape borders. Durable, stackable pieces with natural earth tones that blend seamlessly into outdoor spaces.

Blue Split

Cool blue-gray tones with split faces. Used for walls, edging, and landscape accents with a bold natural texture and clean, durable finish.

Hoosier Select Stone  /  Indiana Limestone

Indiana Limestone

A naturally quarried stone widely used in landscaping and masonry for its durability, versatility, and classic appearance. Proven performance in Midwest climates.

Indiana Limestone offers natural earth tones that blend well into outdoor spaces. Commonly used for patios, walkways, wallstone, steps, and landscape accents. A long-lasting material that suits both residential and commercial projects. Available in wallstone, veneer, and steps.

Available Types

Wallstone Veneer Steps

Product Types

Three products. Timeless material.

Wallstone

Durable, naturally quarried stone for retaining walls, garden walls, and landscape borders. Natural earth tones, stackable shapes, dry-stack or mortared applications.

Veneer Limestone

Thin-cut natural stone for the appearance of full-depth limestone with reduced weight. Applied to fireplaces, facades, columns, and accent walls — interior or exterior.

Natural Steps

Thick-cut Indiana Limestone pieces for stairways, grade changes, and entry transitions. Classic gray tones with natural earth variation. Substantial weight and strength built for long-term outdoor performance. Also available sawn top and bottom — see Landscape Stone > Steps.

Durable, low-maintenance stone for landscape beds, drainage areas, walkways, and ground cover. Available in Poe Gray and Full Color blends in two size ranges.

Available in POE Gray and Full Color blends, decorative gravel provides clean coverage with natural texture and long-lasting performance. Offered in 1–2″ (4's) and 2–4″ (2's) sizes, it delivers versatility for both decorative accents and functional landscape needs in residential and commercial projects. Sold in bulk.

Available Sizes

1–2″ (4's) 2–4″ (2's)

Options

Two colors. Multiple sizes. Five options.

Poe Gray #4 (1–2″)

Small, naturally rounded Poe Gray stones for landscape beds and drainage areas. Easy to spread, ideal for clean coverage and water flow. Sold in bulk.

Poe Gray #2 (2–4″)

Medium-sized Poe Gray stones for landscape beds, drainage, and erosion control. Adds texture and visual depth. Sold in bulk.

Full Color #4 (1–2″)

Small, naturally rounded Full Color stones in warm earth tones. Clean coverage and water flow for beds and drainage. Sold in bulk.

Full Color #2 (2–4″)

Medium-sized Full Color stones for beds, drainage, and erosion control. Warm tones and natural depth for decorative and functional use. Sold in bulk.

Landscape Stone  /  Edging

Stone Edging

Smaller-cut stone pieces for clean borders and defined transitions. Available in Reclaimed Granite, Okie Blue, Okie Brown, Indiana Limestone, and Tennessee in 4×4″ and 8″ snapped sizes.

Natural Stone Edging creates clean, defined borders around garden beds, walkways, and patio outlines. These stones provide structure while maintaining a natural appearance. Durable and timeless, stone edging adds definition and long-term performance to any outdoor space.

Available Sizes

4″ × 4″ 8″ Snapped

Landscape Stone  /  Steps

Natural Stone Steps

Thick-cut stone for stairways, grade changes, garden paths, and entry transitions. Available in Snapped and Natural finishes across six stone varieties — 3′, 4′, and 5′ lengths.

Available in Arkansas, Tennessee, Okie Brown, Grey Armor, Chestnut, and Indiana Limestone, steps offer a range of natural colors and textures to complement any landscape style. Mixed inventory allows flexibility in size, tone, and application for both residential and commercial projects.

3′ 4′ 5′

Okie Brown Snapped
